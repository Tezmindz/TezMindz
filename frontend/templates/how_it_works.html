Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with Tezz-Mindz.

Is Tezz-Mindz aligned with school curriculum (NCERT/CBSE)?
+
Yes! All chapters and concepts across Classes 1 through 8 strictly adhere to NCERT/CBSE curriculum standards while adding competitive rigor for Olympiads like IMO, NSO, and IEO.
How do the 3D games help students learn faster?
+
Our 3D games (such as Dream House Builder and Pizza Master) transform abstract mathematical formulas and scientific processes into concrete visual interactions. Instead of memorizing fractions, students physically assemble parts, dramatically improving conceptual retention.
What makes the AI Little Teacher (TezBuddy) different?
+
Unlike generic chatbots, TezBuddy is specifically programmed with pedagogical Socratic reasoning. When a student chooses an incorrect answer, TezBuddy detects the root misconception and asks guiding questions to help the student derive the correct answer on their own.
Can my child study at their own pace?
+
Absolutely. Chapters unlock progressively as the student demonstrates mastery. Students can revisit past chapters, replay games to achieve higher scores, or jump ahead to advanced Olympiad challenges anytime.
